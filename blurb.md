# Bad Preppers

## Back cover

John Stevens approves software for the power grid. He does not read it. A machine writes the code, John clicks *Looks good to me*, and he goes home to his rifles. He is certain the world is about to end. He is right.

When the power goes out for good, John and his wife, Sanda, discover that fourteen thousand rounds of ammunition, a box of barbed wire and seven Christmas trees do not add up to a plan. Neither does the family that lands in their dark two-bedroom apartment: his retired parents, two grandmothers who have seen worse, and a very small dog.

*Bad Preppers* is a comedy about being bad at the end of the world, and about the one thing nobody planned for: everybody else was just as unprepared.

## Short version (online listings, catalog copy)

John Stevens finds out the hard way that prepping for the apocalypse is not easy. When the lights go out for good, he, his wife, his parents, two grandmothers and a very small dog face the end of the world the only way they know how: badly.
